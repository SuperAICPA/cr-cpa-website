Privacy Policy

Effective November 5, 2026

This policy explains what information I collect through this website, dkcpala.com, and what I do with it. I am Danny Kim, a California CPA, and I operate this site for my practice, CR Accountancy & Consulting.

Information I collect

When you use the contact form, I receive the name, email address, phone number, service of interest and message you type in. If you email or call me, I receive whatever you include in that email or call.

Like most websites, the site also receives technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, device type, the page you came from and the pages you view.

Please do not send Social Security numbers, tax returns or other tax documents through the contact form or ordinary email. If we work together, I will give you a secure way to send them.

How I use it

  • To reply to your inquiry and, if you become a client, to provide the services you engage me for.
  • To see how many people visit the site and how they find it, so I can improve it.
  • To measure whether my Google ads lead to inquiries.

I do not sell your personal information, and I do not share it with anyone for their own marketing.

Third parties that handle information for this site

  • Formspree processes contact form submissions and forwards them to my email.
  • Google provides my email (Gmail) and the fonts used on the site. The Google reviews shown on the site come from Google. If Google Ads conversion tracking is turned on, a Google tag sets a cookie so Google can tell me whether someone who clicked an ad later sent the contact form.
  • Vercel hosts the site and provides visitor statistics (Vercel Web Analytics). Vercel reports totals such as page views and referring sites; it does not use cookies for this.

Each of these companies handles information under its own privacy policy.

Tracking across websites and Do Not Track

When the Google Ads tag is active, Google may collect information about your activity on this site and on other websites over time. You can limit ad personalization in Google's My Ad Center (myadcenter.google.com) and you can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. This site does not change how it works in response to browser Do Not Track signals, because there is no common standard for them.

Client information

Information you give me as a client, including tax return information, is handled under the confidentiality rules that apply to California CPAs and under federal law on tax return information (Internal Revenue Code section 7216). Those rules and our engagement letter govern client information. This policy covers the website only.

How long I keep it

I keep contact form messages and emails as long as I need them to respond to you and for my business records.

Security

The site and the contact form use encrypted (HTTPS) connections. No method of sending information over the internet is completely secure, which is why I ask that you not send sensitive documents through the form.

Reviewing or changing your information

To see, correct or delete the information you sent me through this site, email me at info.dkcpa@gmail.com. I may need to keep some records when the law or professional rules require it.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If I change this policy, I will post the new version on this page with a new effective date.
